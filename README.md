### Business Dashboard Insights

The Power BI dashboard provides an overview of sales, profitability, units sold, products, countries and business segments.

The business generated approximately **$118.73 million in total sales** and **$16.89 million in profit**, resulting in an overall profit margin of approximately **14.23%**. A total of approximately **1.13 million units** were sold.

The **Government segment** contributed the highest sales and profit, while the **Enterprise segment** generated significant sales but recorded an overall loss of approximately **$0.61 million**, indicating an area that may require further cost and pricing analysis.

Among products, **Paseo** generated the highest sales at approximately **$33.01 million**. Sales were also relatively well distributed across countries, with the **United States** recording the highest sales at approximately **$25.03 million**.

The dashboard also shows a substantial increase in sales in **2014**, when sales reached approximately **$92.31 million**, compared with approximately **$26.42 million in 2013**.

Overall, the dashboard helps stakeholders monitor revenue, profitability and sales performance and identify areas requiring further analysis.
